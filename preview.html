Change 3

Nigeria timezone fix

The bug wasn't in how times were displayed — the frontend was already formatting everything correctly in Africa/Lagos, 12-hour time. It was in how the backend stored timestamps: no timezone attached, which could get read back as local browser time instead of UTC, silently shifting every clock by up to an hour.

Change 4

Shift auto-close + forced next-day confirmation

A shift left open past 11:59pm is now closed automatically. The staff member can't open a new shift — or ring up a sale — until they confirm what was actually counted in the drawer for the missed day.
